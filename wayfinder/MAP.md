## Destination

Become comfortable enough in Swift/SwiftUI to build simple touch games on your own — proven by shipping one finished game (a toddler whack-a-mole) that runs on the user's iPhone XR and that their 2-year-old actually plays.

## Notes

Domain: Swift + SwiftUI on macOS via Xcode, targeting iPhone XR (and an iPad available too). The user is a backend engineer, brand-new to Swift — skip beginner-programming concepts; aim straight at Swift/SwiftUI/game specifics. The son is 2–3 years old, so inputs are tap and maybe single-finger drag; keep targets big, no text, no scoring.

Skills every session should consult: `/grilling` + `/domain-modeling` for decisions; `/prototype` for game-design fidelity; `/research` for facts outside the working directory (Apple docs, asset libraries).

Standing preference: keep the first game technically small enough to definitely finish — the goal is *learning Swift*, not shipping a complex game. If a feature can wait, it waits.
