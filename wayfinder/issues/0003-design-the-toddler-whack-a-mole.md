## Question

Raise the fidelity of the first-game idea by making a cheap, concrete artifact to react to. The player is a 2–3 year old on an iPhone XR, tapping with a finger.

Decide with the user:
- One mole at a time, or multiple holes?
- Pop-up timing: how long a hole stays open, how fast it speeds up (or does it stay constant — toddlers don't need difficulty ramps).
- What a hit does: sound, scale-pop, color flash, happy face — and what a *miss* (tapping empty screen) does (probably: nothing, so it's never frustrating).
- No score, no lose-condition, no text — confirm.
- Number and size of holes, big enough targets for an unsteady finger.
- Theme: "mole" literally, or shapes/friendly faces (a 2-year-old may not care about moles).

Deliver a rough prototype (a stub SwiftUI view or a written spec/scenario the user reacts to) linked as an asset. Use the `/prototype` skill. This is HITL.
